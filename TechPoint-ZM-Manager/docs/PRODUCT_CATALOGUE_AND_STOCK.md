# Product Catalogue and Stock — Owner Guide

## The catalogue belongs to the business owner

The app starts with an empty catalogue. RSM TechCare does not need to add every product into the code. The owner can add a product whenever a new phone model or accessory arrives.

The Category field is free text with optional suggestions. Example categories include:

- iPhones
- Android phones
- Pouches & cases
- Chargers & cables
- Screen protectors
- Power banks
- Audio
- Smart watches
- Other

The owner can type a completely new category at any time.

## Add a quantity-managed product

1. Sign in and open Inventory.
2. Tap **Add product**.
3. Enter product name (for example, `Clear iPhone 13 pouch`).
4. Enter or leave the SKU blank for an auto-generated stock code.
5. Type a category (for example, `Pouches & Cases`).
6. Choose **Accessory** or **Other** as the item type.
7. Enter buying cost, selling price, opening quantity and low-stock threshold.
8. Save. The product is now available for checkout.

Use **Adjust stock** when more units arrive or stock is damaged/missing. Enter the reason each time.

## Add an individually tracked phone

1. Tap Add product.
2. Enter the phone model (for example `iPhone 13 128GB`).
3. Choose Phone and enable IMEI tracking.
4. Save the model; the stock quantity starts at zero.
5. Tap Register phone. Record each physical phone separately with its IMEI, serial, storage, colour, battery health, condition and buying cost.
6. Check the IMEI carefully. Duplicate IMEIs are rejected by the database.

Do not store passwords, Apple ID credentials, passcodes or other customer secrets in the phone notes or IMEI record.

## Change or discontinue products

Use Edit for names, categories, prices and reorder levels. Use Archive for items the shop no longer sells. Archive rather than deleting so old receipts and product history remain meaningful.

## Price and profit discipline

Enter the actual buying cost for each item/device. Reports use saved buying cost to estimate gross profit. If the owner does not know a cost, do not guess; enter it only after verifying it. Profit reports are management estimates, not formal accounts.

## Stock intake templates

The CSV templates in this folder help the owner prepare a list before entry. They are worksheets; the current app does not have a bulk CSV import screen. Add the records through the app unless a separate import task is agreed and tested.
